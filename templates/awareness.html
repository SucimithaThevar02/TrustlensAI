🛡️ Scam Awareness

Learn how common digital scams work and discover simple ways to protect yourself from online fraud.

🏦

Banking Scams

Scammers may pretend to be from a bank and claim that your account is blocked or needs verification.

⚠️ Never share your banking password, PIN or OTP.
📱

UPI & Payment Scams

Fraudsters may send fake payment requests or pretend that you need to scan a QR code to receive money.

⚠️ Remember: receiving money does not require entering your UPI PIN.
🔐

OTP Scams

Scammers may pretend to be customer support and ask for an OTP to "verify" your account.

⚠️ Never share an OTP with anyone.
🪪

KYC Scams

Fake messages may claim that your KYC has expired and ask you to click a link or provide personal information.

⚠️ Verify KYC requests through the official bank or service website.
💼

Fake Job Scams

Fake recruiters may promise easy jobs, high salaries or work-from-home opportunities and ask for money or personal information.

⚠️ Be careful with jobs that demand upfront payments.
🎁

Prize & Lottery Scams

You may receive messages claiming that you have won a prize even though you never entered a contest.

⚠️ Unexpected prizes and urgent payment requests are major warning signs.
💰

Investment Scams

Scammers may promise guaranteed profits, unrealistic returns or secret investment opportunities.

⚠️ No legitimate investment can guarantee unlimited profits.
🎣

Phishing & Fake Links

Fake websites and messages may imitate banks, shopping sites or other trusted services to steal information.

⚠️ Check links carefully before opening them.
📲

Social Media Scams

Fake profiles, giveaway messages and suspicious links can be used to trick users into sharing information.

⚠️ Don't trust unexpected messages from unknown accounts.

🛡️ Stay Safe Online

🔹 Never share OTPs, passwords or PINs.
🔹 Don't click suspicious links.
🔹 Verify messages through official websites or phone numbers.
🔹 Don't send money because someone creates urgency or fear.
🔹 Check the sender before trusting a message.
🔹 If something feels suspicious, stop and verify before taking action.
