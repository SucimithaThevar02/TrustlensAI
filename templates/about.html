About TrustLens AI

TrustLens AI is an intelligent digital safety platform designed to help people identify suspicious messages, detect potentially dangerous links, understand scam risks and stay protected from online fraud.

🤖

What is TrustLens AI?

TrustLens AI combines artificial intelligence, scam detection, multilingual analysis, OCR, speech recognition and link analysis to provide users with a simple way to evaluate potentially suspicious digital content.

🔍 What TrustLens AI Can Do

Multiple layers of protection for everyday digital communication.

🔎

Scam Detection

Analyze suspicious messages and estimate their scam probability, risk level and potential warning signs.

🔗

Fake Link Detection

Examine suspicious URLs and identify characteristics that may indicate phishing, unsafe websites or fraudulent links.

🌐

Multilingual Support

Understand and analyze content across English, Hindi, Marathi and mixed-language communication.

🎙️

Audio Analysis

Convert speech into text using speech recognition and analyze the resulting message for scam indicators.

🖼️

Screenshot Analysis

Extract text from screenshots using OCR and analyze suspicious conversations, messages and notifications.

🚩

Scam Reporting

Users can report suspicious messages and contribute to a stronger digital fraud awareness ecosystem.

⚙️ How It Works

Simple steps to understand a suspicious message.

1

Submit Content

Enter a message, upload a screenshot, provide a link or submit an audio recording.

2

AI Analysis

TrustLens AI analyzes the submitted content for suspicious patterns and scam indicators.

3

Safety Result

Receive a risk level, scam probability, reasons and practical safety recommendations.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make digital safety easier, more accessible and more understandable. TrustLens AI aims to help users pause, verify and think before clicking links, sharing information or sending money to unknown sources.
